Rank and price are scraped from Amazon's Renewed best sellers. Units/mo is estimated from within-category rank — the dot is confidence: green above rank 20, amber to 50, red below. Amazon fees and Net sale are PER UNIT: what one sale earns after Amazon's referral and FBA fees. They are not multiplied by quantity. Net sale does not subtract what you paid for the device, so it is not profit. FBA is estimated from an assumed weight per category. Monthly totals are in feed.json and the CSV if you want them. Updates are manual: the numbers change only when the collector is told to refresh, never on a schedule. Refresh data opens that collector, which runs on one machine — it does nothing from anywhere else. Raw data: feed.json next to this page.
